How it works

  1. Reads the website itself. It follows the site's own sitemap, then reads every development and home page politely, one page a second and respecting robots.txt. It refreshes every night.
  2. Understands what people ask. Claude AI turns "something near the station under £350k with a garden" into filters: location and distance, bedrooms, budget, style, offers and must-have features.
  3. Answers from the real page. Before it talks about a home, it re-reads that home's page, so prices and availability are current. Every answer links to the official page to book.

Search by conversation

Town, postcode or "within 10 miles of…", plus bedrooms, budget, house style, Part Exchange, Deposit Boost, ready to move, garage, study and more.

Every detail of every home

Room-by-room dimensions, floor plans, energy features, offers, nearest show home, local schools and transport.

One line to install

A single <script> tag adds the chat to any website: WordPress, Webflow, Shopify or custom.

Honest by design

It only states what the website says, explains when something isn't listed, and hands over to the sales team for anything else.
